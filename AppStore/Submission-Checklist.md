# DayDream App Store Connect submission

## Prepared build

- Bundle ID: `com.daydream.editor`
- Version: `1.1.0` (build `2`)
- Minimum macOS version: 14.0
- Category: Productivity
- Archive: `build/DayDream-AppStore.xcarchive`
- Upload package: `build/AppStoreExport/DayDream.pkg`

## App Store Connect fields to complete

- Name, subtitle, category, price, and availability regions
- Privacy policy URL and support URL
- App description, keywords, and promotional text
- macOS screenshots for the required display sizes
- App privacy declaration: DayDream stores notes locally and does not collect or transmit user data
- App Review contact details and reviewer notes

## Upload

Open Xcode Organizer, select `DayDream-AppStore.xcarchive`, choose **Distribute App**, then **App Store Connect** and **Upload**. After processing finishes, select build `1.1.0 (2)` in App Store Connect and submit it for review.
