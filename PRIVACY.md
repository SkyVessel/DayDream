# DayDream Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 10, 2026**

DayDream is a local-first Markdown writing application for macOS.

## Data collection

DayDream does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. It does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or user accounts.

## Your content

Notes, media attachments, imported documents, and application preferences are stored locally on your Mac. DayDream does not upload this content to a developer-operated server.

If you choose to open a web link, your Mac opens that link using the system browser. The privacy practices of that website are governed by that website's own policy.

## Third-party services

DayDream does not integrate with third-party advertising, analytics, or data-broker services.

## Children

DayDream does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a revised effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, please open an issue at [SkyVessel/DayDream](https://github.com/SkyVessel/DayDream/issues).
